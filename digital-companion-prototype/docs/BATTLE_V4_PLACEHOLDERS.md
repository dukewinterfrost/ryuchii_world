## Broader-content gate

Before expanding the move roster or commissioning replacement art, attach focused automated results and a human playtest record for threat recognition, early/timely/late defense, charge cancellation, opening punishment, useful autonomous behavior, and a desire to try another response. Record actual build/content revision, seed, stats, platform/layout, observations, and the next adjustment. Do not invent observations or mark this gate passed from compilation alone.
