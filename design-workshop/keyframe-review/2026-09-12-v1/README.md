Each Figma column includes the downloaded Digimon Up sprite sheet, labeled candidate poses, and space for comments. Existing workshop pages and runtime assets are preserved.

All 18 PNGs are saved in this directory at 1254 × 1254. [manifest.json](manifest.json) records every final asset path, source reference, generation prompt, Figma IDs and verification results. [figma-nodes.json](figma-nodes.json) maps the review cards. Botamon's final background correction is also recorded in the manifest.

The images are visual concepts for approval. Botamon has an opaque warm-white review background; the other 14 poses have alpha. Production will require final pixel-grid cleanup, consistent canvas/pivots and animation timing after approval. Infant eating and Agumon's additional battle poses replace existing gameplay fallbacks with dedicated proposed poses. Pepper Breath is a body pose; projectile/effect animation remains separate.

Review identity, silhouette, eyes/ears, palette, pixel density and action readability. Full animations should begin only after John approves or revises this set.
